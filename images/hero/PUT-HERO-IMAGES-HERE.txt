Place your hero slideshow images in THIS folder (images/hero/).

Each slide needs 3 files:
  hero.jpg     hero@2x.jpg     hero-mob.jpg
  hero1.jpg    hero1@2x.jpg    hero1-mob.jpg
  ...
  hero10.jpg   hero10@2x.jpg   hero10-mob.jpg

Recommended sizes:
  base  = 1920 x 1080
  @2x   = 2560 x 1440
  -mob  = 900 x 1200 (portrait)
Keep each JPG under ~700 KB.
