Size Guide

Find your fit for jeans and tops. All measurements in inches. Prefer an exact fit? We do custom tailoring — just tell us on WhatsApp.

📐 How to measure

  • Waist: Around the narrowest part of your waist, comfortably snug.
  • Hip: Around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Inseam / Length: From the crotch seam down to the hem (jeans).
  • Chest / Bust: Around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms.
  • Shoulder: Across the back, seam to seam.

Between two sizes, or want it perfect? Choose Custom measurements on any style, or message us directly.
